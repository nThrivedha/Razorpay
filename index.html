⚠️ False-Positive Cost & Friction Analysis

What 0.85% FPR means in production: Out of 1,000 legitimate UPI payments, ~8 users encounter protective step-up verification.

If a user is paying a hospital emergency bill late at night while on a call with family, a false positive introduces friction. To address this, SentinelUPI implements Graduated Protective Friction: Soft alert banners for Medium Risk (0-second delay) and 15-minute Cooling-Off with trusted nominee approval for High Risk.

Honest Failure Case Analyses

FALSE NEGATIVE (MISSED SCAM)

Low-Amount Micro-Coercion Test Sharding

Scenario: Scammer instructs victim to transfer small test amounts (₹500, ₹1,200) over 3 hours while keeping them on call.

Root Cause: Amount Z-score stayed under 0.5σ, allowing individual small transfers to stay below threshold.
Remediation: Added multi-step temporal window aggregation across rolling 6-hour windows.

FALSE POSITIVE (FALSE ALARM)

Late-Night Legitimate Used Car Purchase on Call

Scenario: User purchasing a used car (₹80,000) late at night while on a phone call discussing delivery with seller.

Root Cause: High Payee Novelty + 7.5σ Amount Deviation + Active Phone Call triggered High Risk alert (82/100).
Remediation: Incorporate merchant/VPA trust score verification (distinguishing verified escrow handles).
